What to clarify before signing

Property management agreements can differ, so check the details rather than assuming every company handles the same tasks. Use this short checklist when you speak with a manager:

  • Which services are included, and which would be separate?
  • How are repairs or other property concerns brought to your attention?
  • What choices remain yours as the owner?
  • How will you review activity and understand decisions about the home?
  • What steps are needed to begin, and what information should you provide?

A useful conversation should make each responsibility clear. Gather basic details about the property, your priorities, and any current concerns. Ask for the proposed terms in writing, then read them carefully before you commit. This gives you a chance to spot different expectations early.
