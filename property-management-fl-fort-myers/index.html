A simple checklist for your first discussion

A little preparation can help you get useful answers without needing to know every detail in advance. Gather the information you have about the property, then make a short list of questions. The conversation should help you understand the scope of the service and whether it matches your expectations.

Consider bringing up these points:

  • What outcomes are you hoping for from the property?
  • Which responsibilities are you finding difficult to manage?
  • What decisions do you want to make yourself?
  • How will updates and questions be handled?
  • Which services are included, and what needs a separate arrangement?

One common mistake is choosing based on a broad promise without defining what it means for your specific home. Another is waiting until a concern grows before asking how it should be addressed. Clear questions now can make expectations easier to manage later.
