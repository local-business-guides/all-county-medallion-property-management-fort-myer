Prepare before you hand over responsibilities

Use a short checklist to organize the first conversation:

  • Note the property address, its current condition, and any work you know needs attention.
  • Write down the tasks you currently handle and which ones you hope to delegate.
  • Gather relevant property records and the contact details for anyone already involved.
  • List your financial priorities and any questions about how management decisions are made.
  • Ask what the management agreement covers, how updates are shared, and what may require your approval.

Avoid assuming that a manager will handle every issue automatically. A common mistake is signing an agreement before you understand its scope, communication process, and the decisions you will still need to make. Read the terms carefully and ask for clarification when something is unclear.
