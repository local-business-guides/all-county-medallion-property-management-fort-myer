A checklist for choosing a manager

Use these points to keep the conversation focused:

  • Describe your property and what you currently handle yourself.
  • Ask what the management agreement includes and what falls outside its scope.
  • Find out how updates and questions are handled, and who your contact would be.
  • Discuss how decisions are made when a property issue comes up.
  • Review any proposed terms carefully before agreeing to them.

One mistake to avoid is comparing companies without checking whether they are discussing the same responsibilities. Another is expecting a manager to know your priorities without you explaining them. Share what matters to you, and ask for specific answers about the working relationship before choosing.
